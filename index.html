Photo quality
  • Landscape (16:9) works best — that is the shape of the photo area.
  • At least 1200 × 675 px; the keepsake exports each photo at about 760 × 428 px, so anything larger stays sharp.
  • Sharp, well-lit JPEG or PNG. Screenshots of photos and heavily compressed images look soft.
  • Higher resolution generally gives a better result — the photo is cropped to fill the area, so keep the important part near the centre.
  • This is guidance for your own photos — the Dubai gallery photos are already high-resolution originals.
